## Why control for season?

Naive comparison of rainy vs dry hours with the same mode, hour and weekday, without month fixed effects:

| Mode | Naive rainy-hour change |
|---|---|
| Rail (metro, tram, Marmaray) | -5.9% |
| Road (bus, metrobus) | -5.3% |
| Sea (ferries) | -25.9% |

The naive gap lumps all rain intensities together and mixes rain with the cold and the season it comes with; the model separates intensity, temperature, wind and month.
